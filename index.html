One phone, placed once

The app shows where to put your phone for each stroke. Then you just play.

  • Landscape, still, about waist height
  • Your whole body and racket in the picture
  • A voice tells you when you are in frame, since you can't see the screen from the court

Every main stroke

Right- or left-handed, one- or two-handed backhand.

Forehand

Shoulder turn, contact in front, racket drop and finish.

Backhand

One- or two-handed, compared with a pro who hits it the same way.

Slice

A high take-back and a high-to-low swing through the ball.

Serve

Toss and trophy, racket drop and how high you reach at contact.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Everyone gets 6 analyses a month, and the count starts again on the 1st. A short ad plays while the server builds your 3D swing.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password.

What happens to my video?

Only the stroke you pick (about 3 seconds, without sound) is sent to our server to build the 3D model. It is deleted as soon as your result is ready. Your results stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

What do I need?

An Android phone, a way to stand it up (a small tripod, a bag or the net post) and an internet connection. Mobile data is fine: a swing is a few MB each way.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, in the app: Settings, then Delete my data. See how to delete your data.
