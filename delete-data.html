Delete your ProvenSwing data

ProvenSwing is operated by Qiang Qian. You can delete your data yourself in the app, with no email or password needed.

In the app

Open Settings (the gear at the top of the Train screen), tap Delete my data and confirm. This deletes:

  • every swing, 3D result and your progress history on your phone;
  • your anonymous ProvenSwing account on our server, with the analysis counts linked to it.

Deletion is permanent. The next time you analyse a stroke, the app starts a new, empty anonymous account.

One record stays for a short time: the number of free analyses used on your phone this month. It is linked to a scrambled device identifier, not to an account, and is kept so that deleting cannot reset the monthly free allowance. It is deleted after the end of the following month.

To remove only your swings and keep the account, use Settings, Delete all my swings instead.

Videos

We do not keep your videos. The stroke you upload is deleted from our server as soon as your result is ready.

If you no longer have the app

Uninstalling removes everything stored on your phone. Your anonymous account has no name or email, so we cannot tell which one was yours without the app. If you reinstall the app on the same phone, use Delete my data there. For any other request, email support@provenswing.com. Do not send passwords or codes.

Advertising data

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To reset or delete your advertising ID, open your phone's settings (Google, then Ads). See the privacy policy for more.
