ProvenSwing privacy policy

Updated 18 November 2026.

ProvenSwing is a tennis app operated by Qiang Qian. You film a stroke; the app builds your swing in 3D and shows it next to a professional's. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect and why

  • The stroke you choose. Only the part of your video you pick (about 3 seconds, with the sound removed) is uploaded to our server to build the 3D model. It is deleted as soon as your result is ready. If an analysis fails, its temporary files are removed when the server's worker shuts down, within minutes. We do not keep your videos, share them or use them to train models.
  • Settings sent with the stroke: the stroke type, whether you play right- or left-handed, your backhand style, the 3D avatar you chose, and technical details of the clip (such as the camera's field of view and the moment of contact). They are used for that analysis only.
  • An anonymous account. The app signs in without a name, email or password. The account is a random identifier, used to check that requests come from the app.
  • A device identifier. The app sends a one-way scrambled form (a hash) of your phone's Android ID, and the server scrambles it again before storing it. We use it only to count the free analyses each phone gets per month, so that reinstalling the app does not reset the allowance.
  • Analysis counts: how many analyses were used each month.
  • Connection information, such as IP addresses, which our service providers process to deliver the service and prevent abuse.

Advertising

The free app shows an ad from Google AdMob while the server builds your 3D swing. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about the ads you see, to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in the app under Settings, Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What stays on your phone

Your full videos, your 3D results, your progress history and your settings are kept on your phone only. We have no copy of them.

Service providers

  • Modal (cloud computing): runs the 3D analysis.
  • Supabase: the anonymous sign-in and the monthly analysis counts.
  • Google AdMob: advertising, as described above.

They receive only the information needed for those services. Data is sent over encrypted connections. We do not sell your information, and the app has no third-party analytics.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded strokes: until your result is ready (minutes).
  • Monthly analysis counts: deleted after the end of the following month.
  • The anonymous account: until you delete your data in the app.

Your choices

In the app, Settings, Delete all my swings removes your results from your phone, and Delete my data also removes your anonymous account from our server. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. See how to delete your data.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Email us to make a request.

Children

ProvenSwing is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

We will post changes to this policy on this page with a new date. For privacy questions or requests, email support@provenswing.com.
